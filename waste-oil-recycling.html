Quick Answer

What is Waste Oil Recycling?

Waste oil recycling is the industrial process of collecting recoverable used oils, removing moisture, sediments, and degradation byproducts, and restoring them into valuable reusable base oils or alternative industrial fuels.

Unlike simple disposal, recycling retains valuable hydrocarbon molecules in productive economic use. By treating suitable used industrial lubricants through organized thermal and vacuum distillation, Green Way Industries supports corporate ESG targets, lowers dependency on virgin crude petroleum, and helps Tamil Nadu industries uphold zero-discharge sustainability objectives.

Sustainability & Economic Value

Why Waste Oil Recycling Matters for Modern Industry

Petroleum oil never truly wears out; it simply gets dirty and contaminated. Recycling turns a major environmental liability into a sustainable industrial asset.

Circular Economy & ESG

Support your company's Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) and sustainability scores by redirecting recyclable oil streams into closed-loop re-use.

Conserve Natural Resources

Producing re-refined base oil requires significantly less energy and crude oil compared to refining virgin petroleum from ground reserves.

Auditable Compliance

Ensure full statutory tracking under Hazardous Waste Management Rules with certified recycling receipts and Form-10 documentation.

Our Waste Oil Recycling Process

01

Sourcing & Batch Profiling

We test feedstock samples for water content, flash point, viscosity, and chemical contaminants to verify recycling suitability.

02

Organized Bulk Collection

Recoverable oils are lifted from your premises in vacuum tankers or dedicated drums with full transfer manifests.

03

Dewatering & De-asphalting

Physical filtration and controlled heating strip suspended moisture, light fuels, and heavy particulate contaminants.

04

Vacuum Distillation & Polishing

Advanced vacuum distillation separates high-grade lube oil cuts, polishing them into recovered base stocks and fuel fractions.

Quality Certification & Reporting
