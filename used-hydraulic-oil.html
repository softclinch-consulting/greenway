Quick Answer

What is Used Hydraulic Oil Management?

Used hydraulic oil management involves the controlled extraction, contamination assessment, specialized recycling, or safe disposal of hydraulic fluids drained from industrial machinery, presses, and mobile equipment.

Over thousands of machine operating hours, hydraulic fluids degrade under extreme shear, high thermal cycles, particulate ingression, and moisture contamination. If left untreated, degraded fluid impairs machine control valves and damages hydraulic pumps. Green Way Industries evaluates your used hydraulic oil to recover valuable base oil stocks through thermal re-refining or provide authorized hazardous disposal under Form-10 manifests when fluid cannot be reclaimed.

Equipment & Environmental Protection

Why Managing Used Hydraulic Oil Requires Expertise

Hydraulic oil is engineered for high-pressure power transmission; once drained, its high additive content and micro-particulates demand dedicated handling.

High-Pressure Additive Integrity

Anti-wear additives (ZDDP) and viscosity index improvers break down into acidic residues that require specialized chemical separation.

Moisture & Emulsion Challenges

Hydraulic tanks frequently accumulate condensation and water emulsions that must be removed via controlled dehydration processes.

TNPCB Manifest Compliance

Classified as hazardous waste under Category 5.1, spent hydraulic fluids must be transferred under statutory Form-10 tracking.

Hydraulic Oil Types Handled

We process all major grades and formulations of industrial and mobile hydraulic fluids:
