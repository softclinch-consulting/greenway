Quick Answer

How Does Waste Oil Collection Work?

Waste oil collection is the professional logistical service of collecting stored used oils directly from client facilities using specialized vacuum tankers or barrel transport vehicles.

Instead of letting oil drums accumulate on factory floors—which increases spill hazards and occupies valuable shop space—Green Way Industries coordinates scheduled or on-demand pickups. Our trained teams manage on-site pumping, verify containment safety, issue statutory Form-10 transit manifests, and transfer the material directly to our authorized processing facility.
