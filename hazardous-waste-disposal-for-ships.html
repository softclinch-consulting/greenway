Quick Answer

What is Ship Hazardous Waste Disposal?

Ship hazardous waste disposal is the authorized discharge, reception, and onshore treatment of vessel-generated wastes—primarily MARPOL Annex I oily residues, engine room bilge slops, contaminated lubricants, and shipboard chemical wastes.

Under international maritime law (MARPOL 73/78) and India's Hazardous Waste Management Rules 2016, vessels calling at Indian ports cannot discharge oily wastes into harbor or coastal waters. Green Way Industries provides authorized port reception facilities, coordinating berthed vacuum tanker pumping, issuing official MARPOL Reception Certificates, and providing statutory Form-10 manifests for seamless entry into the vessel's Oil Record Book (Part I).

Maritime Compliance & Turnaround

Why Certified Port Reception Matters for Ship Operators

Port State Control (PSC) inspections strictly scrutinize Oil Record Books. Inadequate disposal documentation can cause port detentions, heavy fines, and sailing delays.
