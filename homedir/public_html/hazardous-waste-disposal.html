Hazardous Waste Disposal

Green Way Industries offers reliable and environmentally safe hazardous waste disposal solutions for industries of all sizes. Improper disposal of hazardous waste such as oils, chemicals, and solvents can lead to soil, air, and water pollution while attracting heavy penalties. Our team follows strict TNPCB guidelines to ensure every step — from collection and storage to transportation and final disposal — is carried out with maximum safety and compliance. We provide full documentation and disposal certificates, giving businesses peace of mind while protecting the environment. With our expertise, hazardous waste is managed responsibly, reducing risks and ensuring a cleaner, safer future.

Green Way Industries is a trusted name in the field of waste oil collection and recycling.
