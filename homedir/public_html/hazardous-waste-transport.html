Quick Answer

What is Authorized Hazardous Waste Transport?

Hazardous waste transport is the legally licensed conveyance of dangerous industrial byproducts, contaminated oils, and chemical wastes from generator premises to authorized recycling or TSDF facilities.

Under the Central Motor Vehicles Rules and the Hazardous and Other Wastes Rules 2016, hazardous materials cannot be transported on standard commercial trucks. They require designated vehicles displaying Emergency Information Panels (EIP), trained drivers holding certified hazardous endorsements, on-board spill control kits, and continuous manifest tracking via Form-10. Green Way Industries provides licensed, fully compliant transport logistics ensuring safe transit with zero legal exposure for industrial consignors.
