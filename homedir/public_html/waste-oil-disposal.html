Quick Answer

What is Industrial Waste Oil Disposal?

Waste oil disposal is the authorized process of collecting, containing, treating, and safely managing petroleum and synthetic oil residues that can no longer be reused or re-refined.

Under the Hazardous and Other Wastes (Management and Transboundary Movement) Rules, industrial waste oil cannot be discarded in municipal drains, dumped on land, or handed to unauthorized handlers. Green Way Industries provides an authorized, end-to-end disposal pathway backed by statutory Form-10 manifests, preventing legal penalties and environmental contamination.
