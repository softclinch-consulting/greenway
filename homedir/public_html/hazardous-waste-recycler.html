Quick Answer

What Does an Industrial Hazardous Waste Recycler Do?

An authorized hazardous waste recycler collects, neutralizes, and processes regulated industrial waste streams to recover usable material resources while reducing the volume sent to hazardous landfills (TSDFs).

Governed by the Hazardous and Other Wastes (Management and Transboundary Movement) Rules 2016, industrial byproducts such as oily sludges, spent hydrocarbons, chemical process residues, and contaminated containers require specialized treatment. Green Way Industries provides an authorized, environmentally sound recycling pathway with complete Form-10 tracking, helping industrial plants manage their hazardous liabilities responsibly.

Environmental & Legal Protection

Why Specialized Hazardous Waste Recycling Matters

Improper handling of hazardous waste carries catastrophic environmental consequences and severe legal penalties under the Environment Protection Act.

Strict Regulatory Compliance

Ensure total conformity with TNPCB parameters and Hazardous Waste Management Rules, avoiding closure notices and statutory liabilities.

Reduce Landfill Dependency

Diverting recyclable hazardous fractions from common hazardous waste landfill sites (TSDFs) conserves capacity and cuts long-term costs.

Statutory Manifest Tracking

Every consignment is documented with signed Form-10 manifests, providing complete chain-of-custody verification for regulatory audits.

Hazardous Streams Handled

Waste Streams Managed Under Rules

We evaluate and manage a defined scope of recyclable hazardous industrial streams:

  • Used oil and spent lubricating residues (Schedule I, Category 5.1)
  • Wastes and residues containing oil or petroleum hydrocarbons (Category 5.2)
  • Contaminated metal and plastic drums/containers (Category 33.1)
  • Oily rags, filter media, and absorbent material contaminated with oil
  • Industrial oil-water separator sludges and interceptor residues
